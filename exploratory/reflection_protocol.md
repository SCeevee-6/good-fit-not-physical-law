# Reflection-window test

Before running, predict exact cancellation of any position-odd auxiliary power in a time window centered at a zero crossing of an even-potential conservative oscillator. Compare with a forward window of equal duration, and include quadratic stiffening (Duffing), damping, and a mistimed center. Use crossing x=0, v=sqrt(1.5), half-length 0.25, potentials x²/2 + beta*x⁴/4 with beta=0 and 2, damping 0 and 0.2. Test three odd functions x*v³, sin(3x)*v² and x³*v². Scale each by its forward-window response so that the amplitude-induced shift equals 1 at zero damping. Predict centered leakage below 1e-8 in conservative aligned cases; no exact prediction under damping or timing error. Include x²*v² as an even negative control. This examines a virtual meter on prescribed trajectories; the law-sign interpretation assumes a calibrated signal term.
