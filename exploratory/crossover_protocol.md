# Reset drift crossover, exploratory follow-up

Freeze before executing. With four repeats and pairwise readout correlation r, standard error scales by sqrt((1+3r)/4). A four-timestamp trace has fixed within-trace correlation 0.6. At fixed development-selected starts, compare the worst projected signed margins in whitened units. If repeat and trace margins at r=0 are R and T (positive), the predicted switch is r*=((R/T)^2-1)/3. Predict that if r* is interior, the observed worst-sign mean accuracy ordering on unseen truths changes within one grid step (0.1) of it. These are deliberately distinct objectives: transfer of the prediction is empirical. Report failures including absent switches.

Design uses the full original grid at assumed coupling 0.3, no test labels and no pilot conditioning. Evaluation uses new continuous coefficients and couplings 0.1 and 0.5, with and without an omitted x*v gain term (+0.25 coefficient). This term vanishes at the target state. Both signs require margin 0.08. Compare correlations 0 to 1 by tenths with fixed marginal instrument variance. Separate worst-sample and worst-sign-mean accuracy; these summarize the finite truth bank only. No hardware threshold or continuous-family guarantee.
